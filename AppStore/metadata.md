## Description

Polaroid makes screenshots feel physical.

Drop any image or let Polaroid catch new screenshots from your Desktop. Your screenshot appears inside an authentic instant-film frame, develops from warm sepia into full color, and is ready for a handwritten caption, paper texture, date stamp, and export.

Built for quick sharing:

- Auto-watch Desktop screenshots
- Drag and drop any image
- Four paper textures
- Six ink colors
- Three handwriting fonts
- Optional date stamp
- Save to Pictures, copy to clipboard, or open the share sheet
- Local-only, sandboxed, no accounts, no analytics

Polaroid is a small, single-purpose Mac utility for making screenshots worth sharing.
